# Official course map - learn-ai-leadership-with-phoebe

**Course:** AI + Leadership - how leaders lead in the AI age
**Scope:** Broad leadership - any manager to C-suite, any function - leading people and organizations through AI: augmented decisions, culture, ethics/responsible AI, change + adoption, team enablement, and strategy. This is leadership-as-the-subject, distinct from the leader-track that rides inside every other applied-AI course.
**Arc:** Topic-session, two-track: leader track (a1-a6, senior/exec: strategy, culture, org) + manager track (b1-b10, hands-on people-manager: AI-assisted decisions, comms, planning, feedback, enablement).
**Coverage bar:** ~80% of mapped sources' working content per session; certificates/exec-ed programs stay official.
**Build mode:** course-taking loop PAUSED (built direct from verified sources, no learner-notes step).

## Hard rails (bake into every relevant page)

- **"AI advises, the leader decides and owns it."** Judgment, accountability, and the human impact of a decision never transfer to the model. "The AI said so" is not leadership.
- **Don't outsource judgment or empathy.** AI can structure a decision or draft a message; the reading of people, the values call, and the hard conversation stay human.
- **People-data rail:** never put employee PII, performance data, or sensitive HR information into consumer AI tools; watch for bias when AI touches people decisions (hiring, promotion, performance) - GDPR/PDPA + employment law apply.
