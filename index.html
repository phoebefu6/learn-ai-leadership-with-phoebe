Learn AI + Leadership with Phoebe

Two tracks for leaders in the AI age

A leader track that teaches senior leaders to steer strategy, culture, and ethics as AI reshapes the organization - and a manager track that puts AI to work in the daily craft of leading people: sharper decisions, clearer communication, better planning and feedback. Around 75% of companies now prioritize AI training for their leaders. The winners will not be the ones who use AI the most, but the ones whose judgment it makes sharper.

Manager's playbook
easy - everyone medium hands-on / deciding hardest
The one rule this whole course runs on: AI advises, the leader decides and owns it. The judgment call, the accountability, and the human impact never transfer to the model - and the reading of people, the values, and the hard conversation stay yours. Never put employee data into a consumer AI tool.
